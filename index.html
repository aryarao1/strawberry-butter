Make yourself at home.

Move the girls, cat, and accessories wherever you like. Turn the tabs to visit each room.

Drop an outfit or accessory onto a girl to wear it. Dragging worn clothes moves the girl. Tap a worn piece and choose Take off to remove it, then place it wherever you like. The strawberry clips come off too.

Tap the blanket to lift it. Drag a girl into bed to tuck her in. Tap Chibs to feed her. Bring a girl to the pancake table, or bring Chibs to the strawberry toy. Tap the makeup lids to open them.

For a keyboard: Tab to a piece, use arrow keys to move it, and Enter to select it.
